AttoLang · Research & Engineering

Technical papers for a language built around explicit semantics.

A growing technical series on language design, compilers, memory ownership, actors, concurrency, and portable ahead-of-time compilation.

How to read these papers. They distinguish language design goals from implemented behavior. Each paper identifies the public evidence available at publication time and avoids treating a specification, host-only test, or unexecuted workflow as proof of guest-runtime support.
