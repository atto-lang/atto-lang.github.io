Real compiler. Explicit scope.

This first milestone supports a strict scalar integer subset, not the full AttoLang language. Immutable bindings are the default; let and mut permit rebinding. Actors, imports, I/O, floats, and general functions are not supported yet and produce compiler errors. A failed compilation never produces a guest Wasm artifact.
