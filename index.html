Source → compiler Wasm → guest Wasm

The playground downloads a prebuilt compiler WebAssembly module, compiles your input inside a disposable browser worker, then executes the generated WebAssembly in a separate worker with restricted imports and a deadline.

Actors, general functions, ownership rules, I/O, GPU blocks, and full language parity are not yet supported by the playground bootstrap. The wider AttoLang language design is separate from this limited demonstration.
